About this buffet

The White Arch Couple Table creates an elegant focal point for your wedding. Three white arched panels frame the couple's table, with soft light outlining the arches and a floral arrangement of white flowers following the curve of the central arch.

Gold calligraphy on the side panels and a golden monogram on the central arch add a personal touch, while the curved white table, the pleated pedestals and the golden accessories keep the whole setup harmonious and easy on the eye.

The buffet is designed and installed by the Star Events team to suit your venue.
